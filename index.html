Get to know me

Hello.

I'm a student who's been drawing since 2020 through breaks, restarts, and a lot of learning along the way. Every piece starts as a rough idea before it's coaxed, slowly, into something that feels a little like magic.

I believe good art isn't about being perfect it's about staying curious and letting every sketch, big or small, be a small part of growing into who I want to become.
