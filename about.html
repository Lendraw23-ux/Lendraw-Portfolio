About Me

Behind every brushstroke

Introduction

Lendra Pradianto.

I'm a student and an artist who finds joy in turning little ideas, feelings, and observations into art. Creating has always been a way for me to express myself, explore my imagination, and capture the beauty I notice in everyday life. I'm still learning and experimenting with different styles, but every piece I create is a small part of my journey.

Outside of creating art, I'm simply a student trying to enjoy the little moments of life while growing into the person I want to become. I believe that being an artist isn't about being perfect—it's about staying curious, learning from every experience, and continuing to create.

Journey

A Path in Progress

  • 2020

    Started Drawing

    Picked up a pencil just for fun and began sketching simple ideas and everyday things around me.

  • 2021–2023

    A Quiet Pause

    Drew less often as school and other activities took priority, but never fully let go of it.

  • 2024

    Back to Drawing, More Seriously

    Returned to art with renewed focus, leaning into a realistic style and steadier practice.

  • 2025

    Still Learning, Still Growing

    Kept experimenting with techniques and styles, treating every piece as a lesson.

  • 2026

    First Clients

    Started taking on drawing commissions alongside school, turning a hobby into something shared with others.
